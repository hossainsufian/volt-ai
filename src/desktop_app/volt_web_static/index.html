⚡ Fast brain model
Smaller models answer much faster but know less. On older computers, a 1.5b model is about twice as quick as a 3b one.

Gemini brain (fast, free allowance)

Answers in a second or two from Google's servers, much faster than this computer's own brain. Get a free API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Your messages are sent to Google, and on the free tier Google may use them to improve its products, so keep private things on the ⚡ Fast brain.

Claude brain (for coding)

Use Anthropic's Claude for much stronger coding and app building. Needs your own API key from console.anthropic.com. Paid: each message uses your Claude credit, and your messages are sent to Anthropic.

Backup to Google Drive

Sign in with your Google account to keep your chats and images safe. If your computer is reset, sign in again and they come back.
